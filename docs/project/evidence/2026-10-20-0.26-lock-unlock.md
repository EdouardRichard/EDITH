## What was exercised

- `UnlockCoordinator` swipe path: `wm dismiss-keyguard`.

## Limits

- Pattern locks and non-ASCII passwords are not supported; the settings page refuses them.
- The test used a temporary PIN and was restored to swipe lock; the owner must configure a PIN through the settings UI
  on a real secure lock.
- Shizuku user-service binding is established before lock; a process killed while locked is not covered by this
  evidence.
